The publishing organization · In development

Dimando Publishing

I want the work of publishing to make room for voices beyond my own.

Dimando Publishing is the planned organization being developed around that commitment: helping authors create written work that readers can return to and pass forward.

Dimando Press, the founding imprint

An imprint is the publishing name a group of books carries. Dimando Press is the founding imprint of Dimando Publishing, rooted in the existing work on faith, discipline, endurance, and perspective.

The organization is being developed to support the wider publishing program. The imprint gives its titles a shared publishing identity. This personal journal remains the home of my essays, field notes, and perspective as an author.
