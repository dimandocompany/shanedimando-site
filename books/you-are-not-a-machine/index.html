A book by Shane Dimando

You Are Not A Machine

A grounded book about identity, discipline, focus, and building a life that is not controlled by noise, hurry, or constant output.

Planned for December 2026

You Are Not A Machine is a response to a world that rewards speed, reaction, and constant output. It challenges the quiet assumption that your value is measured by productivity alone.

This book is about slowing down enough to think clearly, recover direction, and build a life that is aligned instead of reactive.

You do not need to become less human to become more effective.

Written from the middle of the work.

This book was written while building companies, systems, habits, and a more intentional life. It is not theory from the outside. It is a field note from inside the tension.

The goal is simple: to give readers something they can return to when the world feels rushed, unclear, or overly demanding.

Publication details

Release is planned for December 2026. The exact date and editions are still to be confirmed. No purchase or preorder is available here.
