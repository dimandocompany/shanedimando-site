Dimando Press

The founding imprint of Dimando Publishing, with an initial focus on faith, discipline, endurance, and perspective.

The goal is to create written work that people return to, not just consume once.

Dimando Press carries the editorial identity of its titles. Dimando Publishing is the planned organization being developed to support the wider publishing program.

The imprint’s full editorial scope is still being developed.
